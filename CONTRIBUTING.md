# Contributing to ArtCraft Office for Nextcloud

Thank you for your interest in contributing to ArtCraft Office for Nextcloud!

## Contribution Guidelines

1. **Code of Conduct**: Be welcoming, respectful, and collaborative.
2. **AI Contributions**: If you use AI tools (such as Copilot, Claude, Gemini, Antigravity, etc.) to assist with code, documentation, or tests, you **must adhere to our [AI Contribution Policy](AI-POLICY.md)**.
   - Every AI-assisted commit must include an `Assisted-by:` trailer (e.g., `Assisted-by: Antigravity:gemini-3.8-flash`).
   - Every AI-assisted pull request must declare AI usage in the description.
   - You remain the legal and moral author of your submission and must understand, review, and test all code.
3. **Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO)**: Ensure your commits comply with the [Developer Certificate of Origin](https://github.com/nextcloud/server/blob/master/contribute/developer-certificate-of-origin). Only humans may certify the DCO.
4. **Testing**: All contributions must be manually tested and verified on a running Nextcloud instance.
